## 摘要
我们考虑无信号交叉口处自动驾驶车辆的协调问题，其中轨迹规划与通行顺序决策本质耦合，导致在实际中难以同时优化。为此，我们提出一种基于分层结构的启发式方法：下层由一系列非线性规划（NLP）组成，在给定通行顺序下优化每辆车的轨迹；上层启发式利用下层 NLP 的灵敏度信息设计新的通行调度方案。在广泛交通场景下的大量数值仿真表明，与先进先出（FIFO）排序相比，我们的策略在保持计算可处理性的同时显著改善了调度效果。

## Abstract
We consider the coordination of autonomous vehicles at unsignalized intersections, where trajectory planning and crossing-order decisions are intrinsically coupled, thereby making their simultaneous optimization intractable in practice. To this end, we propose a heuristic based on a hierarchical structure consisting of a series of nonlinear programs (NLP) in the lower level, which optimizes trajectories of each vehicle for a given crossing order, and a high-level heuristic to design new crossing schedules that exploits sensitivity information from the lower-level NLPs. Extensive numerical simulations across a broad range of traffic scenarios demonstrate that our strategy achieves substantially improved scheduling with respect to first-in-first-out ordering while preserving computational tractability.
