## 摘要
本文提出了一种用于障碍环境中群体导航的去中心化微观控制框架，该框架在给定由高斯混合模型（GMMs）表示的预计算宏观轨迹的情况下运行。为了在智能体间无通信的情况下生成障碍物感知的参考位置，我们首先在 Wasserstein-2 度量下采用多元正态（MVN）分布之间的仿射传输。然后，我们提出一种方向性质量保持变换（DMPT），通过修改径向传输并同时保持方向结构来纳入障碍物影响。基于变换后的参考位置，应用基于局部人工势场（APF）的控制器进行导航。每个智能体独立运行，从而降低通信和计算负担。仿真结果表明，与传统基于 APF 的方法相比，该方法在效率和避障方面有所改善。

## Abstract
This paper presents a decentralized microscopic control framework for swarm navigation in obstacle environments, given a precomputed macroscopic trajectory represented by Gaussian mixture models (GMMs). To generate obstacle-aware reference locations without inter-agent communication, we first employ affine transport between multivariate normal (MVN) distributions under the Wasserstein-2 metric. We then propose a directional mass-preserving transform (DMPT) to incorporate obstacle effects by modifying radial transport while preserving directional structure. Based on the transformed references, a local artificial potential field (APF)-based controller is applied for navigation. Each agent operates independently, reducing communication and computational burden. Simulation results demonstrate improved efficiency and obstacle avoidance compared to conventional APF-based methods
