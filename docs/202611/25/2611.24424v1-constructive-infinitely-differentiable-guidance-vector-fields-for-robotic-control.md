## 摘要
引导向量场（GVF）是机器人路径跟随和多机器人运动协调等任务中的常用框架，但现有方法可能不可微或导数较大，限制了其在需要引导信号导数的控制器中的应用；当 GVF 作为级联控制器的输入时，这种需求很常见。本文提出一种无穷可微的构造性 GVF。所得向量场依赖于一个平滑参数，是 [1] 的推广，并在该参数趋于零的极限下恢复原始 GVF。它还具有简单高效的实现，在离散化下保持平滑性，并可通过使用显著更少的曲线样本在保持较小路径跟随误差的同时减少计算时间。我们建立了主要理论性质，并通过数值和真实世界实验展示了其实际优势。

## Abstract
Guidance vector fields (GVFs) are a popular framework for tasks such as robotic path-following and multi-robot motion coordination, but existing approaches may be non-differentiable or have large derivatives, limiting their use in controllers that require derivatives of the guidance signal, which is common when the GVF is used as an input in a cascaded controller. This paper proposes an infinitely differentiable constructive GVF. The resulting vector field depends on a smoothing parameter and is a generalization of [1], recovering the original GVF in the limit as this parameter approaches zero. It also admits a simple and efficient implementation that preserves smoothness under discretization and can reduce computation time by using substantially fewer curve samples while maintaining small path-following errors. We establish the main theoretical properties and demonstrate its practical advantages through numerical and real-world experiments.
