## 摘要
编队机动控制使多智能体系统能够在受限环境中以集体运动方式协同导航。尽管已有多种编队控制器，但它们严重依赖事先提供的高质量且动态可行的参考轨迹，因此编队运动规划（FMP）至关重要。为解决传统基于约束优化的方案在高维联合轨迹方面面临的计算挑战，本文提出一种混合优化-生成式框架，重点关注能够提供必要空间灵活性的仿射编队。通过将规划任务建模为受约束的最大后验估计，我们对多智能体目标进行解耦，以利用预训练的单智能体扩散先验来实现动态可行性、避障和平滑性。随后，我们将生成过程与投影梯度下降（PGD）交替进行，以严格施加具有安全保证的仿射几何约束。仿真表明，我们的框架能够生成高度平滑、无碰撞且易于被常见分布式控制器跟踪的规划方案，并在多项指标上优于当前最先进的规划器。

## Abstract
Formation maneuver control enables a multiagent system to cooperatively navigate through constrained environments in collective motion. While various formation controllers exist, they rely heavily on high-quality and dynamically feasible reference trajectories provided a priori, making formation motion planning (FMP) essential. To address the computational challenges for high-dimensional joint trajectories faced by conventional constrained optimization-based solutions, this paper introduces a hybrid optimization-generative framework with a focus on affine formations, which provide the necessary spatial flexibility. By formulating the planning task as a constrained Maximum-a-Posteriori estimation, we decouple multiagent objectives to leverage pre-trained single-agent diffusion priors for dynamic feasibility, obstacle avoidance, and smoothness. We then interleave the generative process with projected gradient descent (PGD) to strictly enforce affine geometric constraints with safety guarantees. Simulations demonstrate that our framework generates highly smooth, collision-free plans that are readily trackable by common distributed controllers and outperforms state-of-the-art planners in various metrics.
