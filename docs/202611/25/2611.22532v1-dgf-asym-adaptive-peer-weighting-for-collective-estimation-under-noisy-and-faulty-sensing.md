## 摘要
不可靠传感下的集体估计要求机器人自适应地平衡自身观测与来自同伴的社会信息。我们提出 DGF-Asym，一种去中心化集体估计方法，它在 DeGroot-Friedkin 模型基础上扩展，引入基于回溯预测性能的非对称同伴加权。该方法无需先验传感器噪声统计或显式故障模型即可支持共识形成，同时保持机器人间通信异步。我们在存在噪声观测和时变传感器故障的模拟集体导航中评估 DGF-Asym。与三个基线相比，它能产生更稳定的估计，并更可靠地维持决策准则，但代价是决策时间更长。然而，整体性能取决于故障比例、群体规模以及影响阈值的选取。我们的结果表明，自适应同伴加权在不可靠传感下实现稳定的无模型集体估计方面具有潜力。

## Abstract
Collective estimation under unreliable sensing requires robots to adaptively balance personal observations and social information from peers. We propose DGF-Asym, a decentralized collective estimation method extending the DeGroot-Friedkin model with asymmetric peer weighting based on retrospective predictive performance. The method supports consensus formation without prior sensor-noise statistics or explicit fault models while keeping inter-robot communication asynchronous. We evaluate DGF-Asym in simulated collective navigation under noisy observations and time-varying sensor faults. Compared with three baselines, it produces more stable estimates and more reliably sustains the decision criterion, at the cost of longer decision times. However, overall performance depends on fault ratio, group size, and influence-threshold selection. Our results demonstrate the potential of adaptive peer weighting for stable model-free collective estimation under unreliable sensing.
