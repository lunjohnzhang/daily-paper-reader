## 摘要
我们研究在存在 $f$ 个拜占庭机器人的情况下，平面中 $n$ 个匿名、无记忆移动机器人在刚性移动与轮转调度下的确定性聚集问题。机器人观测所有机器人的位置及其重数，而它们的局部坐标系可能在激活之间发生对抗性变化。目标是所有正确机器人在有限时间内聚集到同一点，并永远停留在该点。我们分析 ToMax，这是一种简单算法：它移动到重数最大的点，当且仅当其当前位置是唯一最大重数点时保持不动。我们证明，只要 $f\le\lfloor\sqrt{n}/2\rfloor$，ToMax 就能实现聚集，从而建立了可实现的拜占庭韧性的 $\Omega(\sqrt{n})$ 下界。

## Abstract
We study deterministic gathering of $n$ anonymous, oblivious mobile robots in the plane under round-robin scheduling with rigid movement, in the presence of $f$ Byzantine robots. Robots observe all robot positions with their multiplicities, while their local coordinate systems may change adversarially between activations. The goal is for all correct robots to gather at a single point in finite time and remain there forever. We analyze ToMax, a simple algorithm that moves to a point of maximum multiplicity and stays still if and only if its current position is the unique maximum. We prove that ToMax achieves gathering whenever $f\le\lfloor\sqrt{n}/2\rfloor$, establishing an $Ω(\sqrt{n})$ lower bound on the achievable Byzantine resilience.
