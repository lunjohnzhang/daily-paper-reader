## 摘要
通信与移动约束下的多机器人探索需要在信息增益与可达性之间取得平衡。我们提出一种分布式语义探索框架，该框架结合了 Dirichlet 建图、预算约束的轨迹选择以及资源感知分布式贪心（Resource-Aware distributed Greedy, RAG）协调。每个机器人对前沿进行聚类，利用单次预算受限的 Dijkstra 搜索计算地形与航向感知的最小代价路径，并根据边际信息增益选择可行路径，该增益由 Dirichlet 熵的期望减少量表示。每个机器人的每轮选择是精确的，团队的联合每轮信息增益至少达到集中式最优的一半，且该界限在有限通信下能平滑退化。十次运行的网格世界消融实验表明，与最短距离前沿选择相比，所提方法在覆盖率、语义 mIoU 和地图确定性上均有提升，同时具有更低的熵和单位距离通行代价。预算敏感性实验表明，中等预算最能平衡路径可达范围与滚动时域执行。在照片级真实感全系统仿真中，与以其原始发表形式运行的五种协调规则相比，所提方法取得了最高的平均覆盖率和语义准确率，同时是唯一遵守每条轨迹移动预算的方法。

## Abstract
Multi-robot exploration under communication and mobility constraints requires balancing information gain with reachability. We present a distributed semantic exploration framework that combines Dirichlet mapping, budget-constrained trajectory selection, and Resource-Aware distributed Greedy (RAG) coordination. Each robot clusters frontiers, computes terrain- and heading-aware least-cost paths with a single budget-limited Dijkstra search, and selects feasible paths by marginal information gain, represented by the expected reduction in Dirichlet entropy. Each robot's per-round selection is exact, the team's joint per-round information gain is at least half the centralized optimum, and the bound degrades gracefully under limited communication. Ten-run gridworld ablations show improved coverage, semantic mIoU, and map certainty with lower entropy and traversal cost per unit distance than shortest-distance frontier selection. Budget-sensitivity experiments show that an intermediate budget best balances path reach and receding-horizon execution. In photorealistic full-system simulation against five coordination rules run in their native published forms, the proposed method attains the highest mean coverage and semantic accuracy while being the only one that respects the per-trajectory mobility budget.
