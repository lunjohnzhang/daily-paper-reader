## 摘要
搜救（SAR）行动越来越多地部署由空中和地面机器人组成的异构团队。然而，传统覆盖方法通常不会将感知到的地形转化为平台特定的可达性，而持续图像交换则带来高通信成本。我们提出一种以边缘为中心、语义感知的覆盖规划框架，该框架集成了空中地形感知、机器人特定的可通行性推理以及载荷高效的语义状态共享。空中观测被转换为紧凑的语义栅格地图，从而实现可达性约束的区域分解和能力感知的覆盖路径，这些路径仅分配每个机器人能力画像所允许的区域。由此产生的感知—共享—规划闭环将语义修正反馈到可通行性推理和重新规划中，形成一种由面向AI原生6G网络所设想的AI使能目标导向通信所激发的应用级机制。在高更新情形下，相对于周期性全地图共享，传输语义修正使应用载荷降至约1/82。在匹配的基准场景中，所提方法实现了91.5%的覆盖率且没有能力不可行的分配，而LS-MCPP为78.8%的覆盖率和21.5%的能力不可行分配率。语义修正更新共享规划状态，而无需重复传输完整地图。

## Abstract
Search and Rescue (SAR) operations increasingly deploy heterogeneous teams of aerial and ground robots. However, conventional coverage methods typically do not translate perceived terrain into platform-specific reachability, while continuous image exchange imposes a high communication cost. We propose an edge-centric, semantic-aware coverage planning framework that integrates aerial terrain perception, robot-specific traversability reasoning, and payload-efficient semantic state sharing. Aerial observations are converted into compact semantic grid maps, enabling reachability-constrained area decomposition and capability-aware coverage paths that assign only regions admitted by each robot's capability profile. The resulting perception-sharing-planning loop feeds semantic corrections into traversability reasoning and replanning, forming an application-level mechanism motivated by AI-enabled goal-oriented communication envisioned for AI-native 6G networks. For the high-update case, transmitting semantic corrections reduces the application payload by a factor of approximately $82$ relative to periodic full-map sharing. Across matched benchmark scenarios, the proposed method achieved $91.5\%$ coverage with no capability-infeasible allocations, compared with $78.8\%$ coverage and a $21.5\%$ capability-infeasible allocation rate for LS-MCPP. Semantic corrections update the shared planning state without requiring repeated transmission of the complete map.
