## 摘要
我们研究了在预算受限的移动故障模型下，由 n≥3 个自主移动机器人组成的集群的相互可见性问题。机器人是不透明的，因此如果三个机器人共线，中间的机器人会遮挡另外两个机器人之间的视线。每个机器人被分配有限的移动预算，以反映其有限的能量，并限制其在执行过程中可移动的总距离。此外，任意数量的机器人可能因移动故障而永久无法移动。目标是设计一种分布式算法，使非故障机器人能够协调其移动，从而在有限时间内，每个非故障机器人都能无遮挡地看到系统中的所有机器人（包括故障机器人），同时遵守规定的移动预算。我们考虑在 SSYNC 模型下运行、具有非刚性移动、对局部坐标系没有任何一致约定且仅配备一个公共固定参考点的发光机器人。我们提出了一种确定性分布式算法，即使存在任意数量的移动故障也能解决该问题。该算法保证非故障机器人的相互可见性，遵守每个机器人的移动预算，为机器人提供无碰撞移动，并且仅使用 12 种光颜色。

## Abstract
We investigate the mutual visibility problem for a swarm of $n\ge3$ autonomous mobile robots under the budget-constrained mobility fault model. The robots are opaque, so if three robots are collinear, the middle robot obstructs the visibility between the other two. Each robot is assigned a finite movement budget, reflecting its limited energy, that bounds the total distance it may traverse during the execution. Moreover, an arbitrary number of robots may become permanently immobile due to mobility faults. The objective is to design a distributed algorithm that enables the non-faulty robots to coordinate their movements so that, within a finite time, every non-faulty robot attains unobstructed visibility of all robots in the system, including the faulty ones, while respecting the prescribed movement budget. We consider luminous robots operating under the $\mathsf{SSYNC}$ model with non-rigid movements, without any agreement on their local coordinate systems, and equipped only with a {\it common fixed reference point}. We present a deterministic distributed algorithm that solves the problem despite an arbitrary number of mobility faults. The algorithm guarantees mutual visibility for the non-faulty robots, respects the movement budget of every robot, provides collision-free movements for the robots, and uses only 12 light colors.
