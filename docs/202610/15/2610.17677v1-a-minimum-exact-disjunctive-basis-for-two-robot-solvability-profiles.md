## 摘要
我们完整刻画了平面中两个自主移动机器人在可变失向、手性和刚性运动下的任务可解性剖面。这里的四种标准能力模型以及所考虑的调度器具有一个已知的商序 Q，其中包含十三个计算类。一个任务的可解性剖面是其中它可解的那些类构成的集合，并且必然是 Q 的一个上闭集。我们证明每个上闭集都是可实现的，并构造了一个由十三个具体任务组成的最小精确析取基。该基对每个主上闭集包含一个任务。对于 Q 的每个反链，相应任务的析取具有的剖面恰好等于它们剖面的并集。这种精确性并不能由各个剖面单独推出：一个算法可能在不同执行中满足不同的析取项。我们通过如下方式确立这一点：对于目标并集之外的每个极大类中的每个算法，构造一个单一的可容许公平执行，同时违反所有被选中的谓词。因此，可实现剖面构成整个有限分配格 Up(Q)：恰好有 54 个剖面，由反链多项式 1 + 13z + 24z^2 + 16z^3 计数，并且每个剖面都有一个使用至多三个主任务的典范析取表示。我们还识别出六个任务，其可解性签名能够恢复 Q 中的每个包含关系和不可比关系，并证明即使是在任意分离任务中，六也是最小值。它们的剖面恰好是由 Q 的并不可约元生成的主上闭集。相反，任何通过精确析取生成所有剖面的任务族都必须包含全部十三个主剖面。因此，我们的精确析取基具有最小规模，并且在剖面层面上是唯一的。

## Abstract
We completely characterize the solvability profiles of tasks for two autonomous mobile robots in the plane under variable disorientation, chirality, and rigid movements. The four standard capability models and the schedulers considered here have a known quotient order Q of thirteen computational classes. A task's solvability profile is the set of classes in which it is solvable, and is necessarily an upset of Q. We prove that every upset is realizable and construct a minimum exact disjunctive basis of thirteen concrete tasks.   The basis contains one task for each principal upset. For every antichain of Q, the disjunction of the corresponding tasks has profile exactly equal to the union of their profiles. This exactness is not implied by the individual profiles: an algorithm may satisfy different disjuncts in different executions. We establish it by constructing, for every algorithm in each maximal class outside the target union, a single admissible fair execution violating all selected predicates simultaneously. Consequently, the realizable profiles form the entire finite distributive lattice Up(Q): there are exactly 54 profiles, counted by the antichain polynomial 1 + 13z + 24z^2 + 16z^3, and each has a canonical disjunctive representation using at most three principal tasks.   We also identify six tasks whose solvability signatures recover every inclusion and incomparability in Q, and prove that six is minimum even among arbitrary separating tasks. Their profiles are precisely the principal upsets generated by the join-irreducible elements of Q. In contrast, any task family generating every profile by exact disjunctions must contain all thirteen principal profiles. Thus our exact disjunctive basis has minimum size and is unique at the profile level.
