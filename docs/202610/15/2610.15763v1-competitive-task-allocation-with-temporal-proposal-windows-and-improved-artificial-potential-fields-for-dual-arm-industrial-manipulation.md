## 摘要
现代工业自动化需要能够在具有异构物体布置的工作空间中进行协同操作的自主协作系统。现有系统依赖预定轨迹和刚性分配策略，导致工作空间利用率次优和碰撞风险。本文提出一种面向双臂系统的基于距离的竞争性任务分配框架，将实时视觉感知与无碰撞运动规划相结合。一种时间窗口机制使每条机械臂在做出基于距离的最优选择之前，对连续五个视觉帧中的物体检测进行评估，并具备动态重新分配和以0.01m阈值精度进行空间冲突消解的能力。改进人工势场（iAPF）框架通过指数目标引力、臂间斥力、归位力和速度阻尼力实现双臂协调运动。仿真结果验证了其在距离效率、碰撞风险和工作空间覆盖率方面优于基于市场、轮询和固定分配策略。使用两个6自由度Kinova Gen3 Lite机械臂的硬件实验表明，在同类场景中，与固定分配相比，平均速度提升约47%，且碰撞为零；而混合物体场景则证实了通用处理能力，约11%的时间开销被更优的安全裕度所抵消。该框架将30 FPS的YOLOv8 OBB检测与10 Hz控制回路集成，展示了面向工业装配和分拣操作的实际部署能力。

## Abstract
Modern industrial automation demands autonomous collaborative systems capable of coordinated manipulation in workspaces with heterogeneous object arrangements. Existing systems rely on predetermined trajectories and rigid allocation strategies, causing suboptimal workspace utilization and collision risks. This paper presents a distance-based competitive task allocation framework for dual-arm systems, integrating real-time vision perception with collision-free motion planning. A temporal window mechanism enables each arm to evaluate object detections over five consecutive vision frames before committing to optimal distance-based selection, featuring dynamic reallocation and spatial conflict resolution at 0.01m threshold accuracy. An improved Artificial Potential Field (iAPF) framework provides coordinated dual-arm movements through exponential goal attraction, inter-arm repulsion, home-seeking, and velocity damping forces. Simulation results validate superiority over market-based, round-robin, and fixed allocation strategies in distance efficiency, collision risk, and workspace coverage. Hardware experiments using two 6-DOF Kinova Gen3 Lite manipulators demonstrate approximately 47% average velocity improvement over fixed allocation in homogeneous scenarios with zero collisions, while mixed object scenarios confirm universal handling with approximately 11% time overhead offset by superior safety margins. The framework integrates YOLOv8 OBB detection at 30 FPS with 10 Hz control loops, demonstrating practical deployment capability for industrial assembly and sorting operations.
