## 摘要
多智能体搜索与覆盖对自主探索、搜救和防御应用至关重要。本文研究移动传感器网络协调发现有界区域内静止目标位置的问题。我们提出一种贝叶斯更新方案，其中智能体通过逆方差加权迭代地融合由其传感器测量得到的局部不确定性，从而在给定某种先验信念的情况下更好地全局理解目标可能所在的位置。网络智能体的轨迹由原始-对偶梯度更新规则控制，该规则同时最小化期望感知不确定性并强制智能体之间的网络连通性。我们证明，在温和且与规划器无关的条件下，信念会收敛到目标，并给出显式的错误假设拒绝指数速率以及期望搜索时间的界。仿真结果表明，我们能够成功找到目标并使信念坍缩到真实目标位置，同时确保传感器网络的连通性。仿真结果也从经验上验证了我们的理论界和重访假设。最后，我们与割草机式扫掠方法进行比较，表明当先验信息具有信息量时，我们的方法能更快定位目标。

## Abstract
Multi-agent search and coverage is critical to autonomous exploration, search-and-rescue, and defense applications. This paper investigates settings where a mobile sensor network is tasked with coordinating to discover the position of a stationary target within a bounded region. We develop a Bayesian update scheme where agents iteratively combine local uncertainties derived from their sensor measurements through inverse variance weighting to get a better global understanding of where the target may be located given some prior belief. Network agents' trajectories are governed by a primal-dual gradient update rule that simultaneously minimizes the expected sensing uncertainty and enforces network connectivity among the agents. We prove that the belief converges to the target under mild, planner-agnostic conditions, with an explicit exponential rate of false-hypothesis rejection and a bound on expected search time. Simulation results show that we can successfully find the target and collapse belief to the ground truth target location while ensuring connectivity of our sensor network. They also empirically confirm our theoretical bounds and revisiting assumption. Finally, we compare against a lawnmower sweep, showing that our method locates the target more quickly when the prior is informative.
