## 摘要
资源受限的移动智能机器（如无人机（UAVs））之间的有效协作对于完成复杂任务至关重要。然而，并发任务需求、有限的分布式资源以及动态变化的协作者可用性，使得在不同协作系统中满足任务特定的完成目标颇具挑战。为同时应对这些挑战，我们提出了一种新的可信协作者群体组建框架及支撑并发任务编排的资源分配策略。具体而言，将协作任务分解为受动态资源可用性和子任务间依赖关系约束的子任务。构建任务效用模型以支持协作者群体评估，从而依据任务完成目标和统一的价值—风险分析支持子任务分配。基于该模型，将并发任务编排建模为α公平性下的长期效用最大化问题。为高效求解该问题，我们提出一种分层匹配策略：先将任务和协作者聚类为粗粒度集合，随后在匹配集合内执行细粒度协作者群体组建与任务分配。任务特定信任通过时空图神经网络（GNNs）预测，并嵌入公平感知深度强化学习（DRL）框架以持续更新匹配策略。仿真结果表明，在资源约束下，所提策略相较代表性基线将并发任务完成性能提升22%。

## Abstract
Effective collaboration among resource-constrained mobile intelligent machines, such as Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs), is essential for the completion of complex tasks. However, concurrent task demands, limited distributed resources, and dynamic collaborator availabilities make it challenging to satisfy task-specific completion goals in diverse collaborative systems. To address these challenges simultaneously, we propose a new framework of trusted collaborator group formation and the supporting resource allocation strategy for concurrent task orchestration. Specifically, a collaborative task is decomposed into subtasks subject to dynamic resource availability and inter-subtask dependencies. A task utility model is developed for collaborator group evaluation support subtask allocation according to task completion goals and unified value-risk analysis. Based on this model, concurrent task orchestration is formulated as a long-term utility maximization problem under $α$-fairness. To solve the problem efficiently, we develop a hierarchical matching strategy that first clusters tasks and collaborators into coarse-grained sets and subsequently performs fine-grained collaborator group formation and task allocation within matched sets. Task-specific trust is predicted through spatial-temporal Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) and embedded into a fairness-aware Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) framework to continuously update the matching policies. Simulation results demonstrate that the proposed strategy improves concurrent task completion performance by 22% over representative baselines under resource constraints.
