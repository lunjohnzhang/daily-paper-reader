## 摘要
同时监视与围捕（SSC）问题出现在周界安防和环境监测等多机器人应用中：机器人需要巡查感兴趣区域，并在检测到目标后协同对目标形成包围。监视倾向于通过空间分散来获得覆盖，而围捕则需要以目标为中心的聚集来实现包围。这种冲突使启发式切换不稳定，并且随着更大规模机器人网络引入更多智能体间和智能体-目标关系，这种不稳定性会更加明显。端到端深度强化学习（DRL）减少了对手工设计切换的依赖，但传统单流策略通常将监视与围捕线索混合在一个共享特征空间中，这可能使面向分散的信号和以目标为中心的信号相互纠缠，并削弱任务转换。本文提出双流门控网络（DSGN），该架构通过将监视与围捕分离到专门的表示流中，并通过上下文感知门控机制协调二者，旨在解决规模放大带来的任务转换困难。概率先验进一步对门控过程进行正则化，减少模糊的中间决策，并在大规模交互复杂性下支持更清晰的行为转换。评估表明，在恒定密度的跨尺度设置下，DSGN保持了较强的任务效能和可扩展性。额外的消融实验和分阶段评估表明，所提出的动态门控机制和概率先验在纯监视和任务切换场景中均支持有效的任务仲裁和可靠的模式切换。源代码可在 https://github.com/zuozysama/DSGN 获取。

## Abstract
The simultaneous surveillance and containment (SSC) problem arises in multi-robot applications such as perimeter security and environmental monitoring, where robots must survey a region of interest and coordinate to enclose targets once they are detected. Surveillance favors spatial dispersion for coverage, whereas containment requires target-centric cohesion for enclosure. This conflict makes heuristic switching unstable, and the instability becomes more pronounced as larger robotic networks introduce more inter-agent and agent-target relations. End-to-end DRL reduces reliance on hand-crafted switching, but conventional single-stream policies often mix surveillance and containment cues in one shared feature space that could entangle dispersion-oriented and target-centric signals and weaken task transitions. In this paper, we propose the Dual-Stream Gated Network (DSGN), an architecture designed to address scale-amplified task-transition difficulties by separating surveillance and containment into specialized representation streams and coordinating them through a context-aware gating mechanism. A probabilistic prior further regularizes the gating process, reducing ambiguous intermediate decisions and supporting clearer behavioral transitions under large-scale interaction complexity. Evaluations show that DSGN maintains strong task efficacy and scalability under constant-density cross-scale settings. Additional ablation and phase-specific evaluations show that the proposed dynamic gating mechanism and probabilistic prior support effective task arbitration and reliable mode switching in both pure surveillance and task-switching scenarios. The source code is available at https://github.com/zuozysama/DSGN.
