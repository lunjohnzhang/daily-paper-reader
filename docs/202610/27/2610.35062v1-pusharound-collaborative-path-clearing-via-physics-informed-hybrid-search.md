## 摘要
在杂乱环境中，大型车辆常被可移动障碍物阻挡，这促使移动机器人团队清理出可通行的走廊。可移动障碍物环境下的导航（NAMO）方法通常对障碍物位移进行抽象处理，而忽略机器人尺寸、障碍物质量、接触几何与力，从而生成物理上不可行的规划。PushAround 是一种物理信息混合搜索方法，它将几何连通性推理与短时域仿真相结合，以构建可执行的清理规划。重复的 W-间隙连通图（WCCG）查询可揭示需要拓宽的前沿间隙，并按估计的下游代价对其进行排序。该搜索生成直接或递归推动候选方案，选择障碍物、方向、接触与力，并接纳经物理评估的候选方案。数值实验将 WCCG 与标准连通性检查进行对比验证，并证明其可扩展到更密集的杂乱环境和最多四台机器人组成的团队，且对仿真执行侧物理参数失配具有鲁棒性。

## Abstract
Large vehicles in cluttered environments are often blocked by movable obstacles, motivating mobile robot teams to clear traversable corridors. Navigation Among Movable Obstacles (NAMO) approaches often abstract obstacle displacements while neglecting robot dimensions, obstacle mass, contact geometry and forces, yielding physically infeasible plans. PushAround is a physics-informed hybrid search that couples geometric connectivity reasoning with short-horizon simulation to construct executable clearing plans. Repeated W-clearance connectivity graph (WCCG) queries expose frontier gaps requiring widening and rank them by estimated downstream costs. The search generates direct or recursive pushing candidates, selecting obstacles, directions, contacts and forces, and admits physics-evaluated candidates. Numerical experiments validate WCCG against standard connectivity checks and demonstrate scaling to denser clutter and teams of up to four robots, with robustness to simulated execution-side physical-parameter mismatch.
