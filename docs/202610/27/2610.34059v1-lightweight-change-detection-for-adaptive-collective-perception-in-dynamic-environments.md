## 摘要
群体机器人通过集体感知实现可扩展的巡检，其中机器人从分布式观测中推断环境属性。一个常见基准考虑机器人探索黑白瓷砖环境并确定多数瓷砖类型。大多数工作研究静态环境，而动态环境仍鲜有探索。我们研究动态环境中的集体感知，其中瓷砖比例和表面图案的空间相关性可能随时间变化。为便于在资源受限机器人上实现，我们将轻量级变化检测机制集成到贝叶斯集体决策框架中，在检测到分布偏移时重置机器人信念。所提出的检测器需要常数内存和常数每样本计算，而两种近期竞争方法会存储完整样本流，因此该方法适合内存受限机器人。我们进一步引入一种方法，用于检测空间相关采样并在线调整采样间隔以缓解相关效应。我们在 Webots 中的仿真结果表明，其变化检测延迟具有竞争力，在不同通信设置下精度更一致，并且在变化的空间相关性下提高了决策准确性。

## Abstract
Swarm robotics enables scalable inspection through collective perception, where robots infer environmental properties from distributed observations. A common benchmark considers robots exploring black-white tiled environments and determining the majority tile type. While most work studies static environments, dynamic environments remain underexplored. We study collective perception in dynamic environments where the tile proportion and the spatial correlation of surface patterns may change over time. To enable implementation on resource-constrained robots, we integrate a lightweight change detection mechanism into a Bayesian collective decision-making framework that resets robot beliefs when distribution shifts are detected. The proposed detector requires constant memory and constant per-sample computation, unlike two recent competitive approaches that store the full sample stream, making it suitable for memory-constrained robots. We further introduce a method that detects spatially correlated sampling and adapts sampling intervals online to mitigate correlation effects. Our simulation results in Webots show competitive change detection delays, more consistent precision across communication settings, and improved decision accuracy under changing spatial correlations.
