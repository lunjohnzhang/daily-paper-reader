## 摘要
给定欧几里得平面中的一组点机器人 $\mathcal{R}$ 以及一个包围所有机器人位置的目标圆 $\mathbf C$，最小-最大均匀圆形成（Min-Max Uniform Circle Formation, MMUCF）问题要求机器人移动到 $\mathbf C$ 上互不相同的位置，使得最终构型形成一个正 $n$ 边形，同时最小化任一机器人所移动的最大距离。均匀圆形成是群体机器人学中的一项基本协调任务，在周界监测、监视、边界覆盖和模式形成等方面具有应用。文献尚未涉及对形成过程中最大个体位移的优化。在本工作中，我们研究圆形成和均匀圆形成问题的最小-最大版本，其目标是最小化任一机器人所移动的最大距离。我们在 $\mathcal{ASYNC}$ 模型下考虑这些问题；在该模型中，机器人是自主的、匿名的、相同的、同质的、无记忆的且静默的，并在非刚性运动下遵循 \textit{Look--Compute--Move} 模型运行。我们首先给出确定性解的必要条件，然后提出确定性、分布式且无碰撞的算法，这些算法在有限时间内形成圆和均匀圆，同时最小化最大移动距离。这些算法确保机器人在圆上到达互不相同的位置，并且在均匀情形下，在所考虑的模型中到达 $\mathbf C$ 上等间距的位置。

## Abstract
Given a set of point robots $\mathcal{R}$ in the Euclidean plane and a target circle $\mathbf C$ enclosing all robot positions, the \textsc{Min-Max Uniform Circle Formation (MMUCF)} problem requires the robots to move to distinct positions on $\mathbf C$ such that the final configuration forms a regular $n$-gon while minimizing the maximum distance traveled by any robot. Uniform circle formation is a fundamental coordination task in swarm robotics with applications in perimeter monitoring, surveillance, boundary coverage, and pattern formation. The literature does not address the optimization of the maximum individual displacement during the formation process.   In this work, we study the min--max versions of the circle formation and uniform circle formation problems, where the goal is to minimize the maximum distance traveled by any robot. We consider these problems under the $\mathcal{ASYNC}$ model, where robots are autonomous, anonymous, identical, homogeneous, oblivious, and silent, and operate under the \textit{Look--Compute--Move} model with non-rigid motion. We first give necessary conditions for a deterministic solution and then present deterministic, distributed, and collision-free algorithms that form a circle and a uniform circle in finite time while minimizing the maximum movement. The algorithms ensure that robots reach distinct positions on the circle and, in the uniform case, equally spaced positions on $\mathbf C$ under the considered model.
